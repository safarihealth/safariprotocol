Why ninety days

The window most fertility advice ignores

A human egg completes its final maturation over roughly ninety days. Sperm form over about seventy-four. The three months before conception are the window in which daily choices — sleep, nutrition, targeted testing, supplementation, environment — can support the cells themselves, in real time rather than in hindsight.

Most fertility advice ignores this window or fills it with generic tips. The S.A.F.A.R.I. Protocol structures it.

The six compass points

Six domains, each a verb, worked in sequence

S

Stabilize

Your rhythms: sleep, circadian alignment, stress regulation.

A

Anchor

With nutrition: anti-inflammatory eating, steady blood sugar, protein at every meal — both partners.

F

Find

The bottlenecks: the testing that shows what a standard workup can miss.

A

Amplify

Cellular energy: tiered, targeted supplementation — deficiencies first, never bottle-stacking.

R

Remove

The interference: everyday toxins, endocrine disruptors, and heat exposure for the male partner.

I

Integrate

With treatment and life: routines that fit a clinic timeline and survive a stressful season.

Who it is for

Three kinds of couple, one framework

For couples trying naturally, couples preparing for IVF, and couples regrouping after a cycle that did not work. The ninety days run in three legs — Establishing Base Camp, Reading the Terrain, and The Final Approach.
