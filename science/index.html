The evidence

Where the evidence stands

No fertility framework should ask for your trust without showing its working.

This page grades the protocol’s own evidence in three honest bands: aligned with current guidelines, supported by good evidence but ahead of guidelines, and hypothesis-level — flagged as such. The full references are in the book and the scientific review.

Stabilize — sleep, circadian rhythm, stress

Sleep and circadian disruption are linked to altered reproductive hormones and cycle irregularity. Guideline position: broadly acknowledged; specific sleep prescriptions are ahead of formal guidelines.

Anchor — nutrition and blood sugar

Mediterranean-pattern eating and glycaemic stability are associated with better fertility outcomes. Guideline position: bodies acknowledge diet matters but none prescribes a specific pattern; the protocol’s structured approach is ahead of consensus.

Find — extended investigation

Studies re-investigating “unexplained” infertility identify pathology in a majority of cases. Guideline position: some elements (3D ultrasound, HyFoSy) align; tighter metabolic and thyroid targets extend beyond current recommendations and are presented as hypotheses.

Amplify — tiered supplementation

Deficiency correction and CoQ10 for diminished reserve have moderate supporting evidence; several meta-analyses show benefit for egg and sperm parameters. Guideline position: deficiency correction supported; frontier agents remain preclinical or conflicting. No trial has tested the integrated protocol.

Remove — environmental exposure

Endocrine-disrupting chemicals and male heat exposure are associated with reduced fertility parameters. Guideline position: acknowledged as relevant; the protocol’s practical high-yield swaps are low-risk and low-cost.

Integrate — alignment with treatment

Coordinating preconception work with a clinic timeline is pragmatic rather than trial-tested. Guideline position: consistent with good clinical practice.

What has not been shown

The honest ledger

  • No trial has tested the integrated protocol as a whole.
  • Several elements rest on observational rather than randomised data.
  • Frontier supplements remain preclinical; they are labelled as such.
  • Some targets deliberately extend beyond current guidelines and are framed as research questions.
